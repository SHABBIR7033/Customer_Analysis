# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis** project explores customer purchasing patterns using transactional retail data. The objective is to identify valuable business insights related to customer demographics, spending habits, product preferences, subscription behavior, and purchasing trends.

By combining **Python**, **PostgreSQL**, and **Power BI**, the project demonstrates a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow—from data cleaning and transformation to SQL-based business analysis and interactive dashboard visualization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Evaluate the impact of subscriptions and discounts on sales.
* Discover top-performing products and categories.
* Generate actionable business recommendations through data-driven insights.

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Cleaning

The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Python before loading into PostgreSQL.

### Data Cleaning Steps

* Imported dataset using Pandas.
* Performed exploratory analysis using:

  * `df.info()`
  * `df.describe()`
* Identified missing values.
* Imputed missing Review Ratings using median ratings grouped by product category.
* Standardized column names using snake_case naming convention.
* Removed redundant columns after consistency validation.

### Feature Engineering

Created additional business-friendly features:

#### Age Group Classification

Customers were grouped into:

* Young Adult
* Adult
* Middle-aged
* Senior

#### Purchase Frequency

Generated a purchase frequency metric to support customer segmentation and behavioral analysis.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer Behavior

* Loyal customers represent the largest customer segment.
* Repeat buyers show stronger engagement and purchasing activity.

### Product Performance

* Gloves and Sandals received the highest customer ratings.
* Jewelry, Blouses, and Sandals were among the most purchased products.

### Subscription Analysis

* Non-subscribers generated the majority of total revenue.
* Opportunity exists to improve subscription adoption.

### Shipping Preferences

* Express shipping users spent slightly more on average than standard shipping users.

### Age Group Contribution

* Young adults contributed the highest overall revenue.
